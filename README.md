# The Paycheck Platform

An animated, single-file walkthrough of a FinTech business case: should **Brisko**, a delivery app with 1.2M couriers, launch **Brisko Pay** (a Crestline Bank debit card with free instant pay, 3% gas cash back and $300 advances), and how?

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no dependencies.

## Scenes

1. **The problem**: a live courier network leaking 9% a month to rival apps, with key numbers and Exhibit 1.
2. **Choose an option**: play each strategy as an animation.
   - **A · Walled garden**: three walls rise, Earnly is cut off, adoption hits 45%, churn falls from 9% to 6%, and a profit waterfall builds to +$70M.
   - **B · Open by design**: no walls; couriers come and go and some come back.
   - **C · Partner with Earnly**: a revenue-share link, no card.
   - **D · Hybrid**: card and advances with no penalty fees, a portable earnings record, a fee cap and a year-1 review.
3. **Compare**: a scorecard across profit, retention, courier welfare, partner trust and regulatory risk, plus why a 3% one-week advance is about 156% a year.

## Editing numbers

Every number lives in the `DATA` object at the top of the `<script>` in `index.html`.

## Controls

Next / Back (or the arrow keys), Replay for each option, and a light/dark toggle.
